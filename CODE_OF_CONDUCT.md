# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project is a small, safety-focused engineering effort. We want feedback,
disagreement, and bug reports — delivered with basic respect.

**Expected:** be direct and technical; critique ideas, code, and claims, not people;
assume good faith; keep discussion grounded in evidence (a command run, a diff, a
log) rather than vibes.

**Not tolerated:** harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory
comments, or deliberately bad-faith engagement.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions that violate this standard, and
may block repeat offenders. To report conduct concerns privately, use GitHub's
private vulnerability reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md), or
contact the maintainer directly.

This is a lightweight adaptation in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
